## Hypotheses checked
- A1 | partly. Confirmed: iOS 27 on-device model accepts image attachments through `Attachment(...)` inside `session.respond {}` (Apple + HF PR validated on an iPhone running iOS 27); larger images consume more tokens and latency (Apple). Not independently confirmed: contextSize == 8192 (Apple sample only; a "4K" reading floats in an unattributed summary); "image tokens count against the window" is an inference.
- A2 | confirmed (Apple x2). Nuance: s375 says "deprecated", news says "will no longer work in iOS 27 ... or later"; s375 also says the sheet API is PCC-based and sourceImage is "a starting point, not a constraint".
- A3 | confirmed with one gap. `sam3` (lite 336x336 default, 32-token text prompts), `flux2` (Klein 4B, 512x512 iOS default, `--single-function`, reference-token img2img) and `stable-diffusion` (1.5 / 2.1 / 3.5 Medium) exist; floor "macOS and iOS 27.0+", "Xcode 27.0+", BSD-3-Clause code. Gap: the 3.5 Medium VAE encoder for img2img. Note weights are separately licensed/gated (SAM 3 gated on HF; SD gated), which the repo licence does not cover.
- A4 | partly. The forum report is exact. Apple DTS (Sept 2025): background GPU is "only supported on iPad's with an M3 or better (and not supported on any iPhone)". Nothing found for iPhone 17 Pro / iOS 27 -> unverified. What would settle it: a device probe on an iPhone 17 Pro with iOS 27, `print(BGTaskScheduler.shared.supportedResources)` and `.contains(.gpu)`, plus a submitted `BGContinuedProcessingTaskRequest` with the GPU resource and the `com.apple.developer.background-tasks.continued-processing.gpu` entitlement (the entitlement name is quoted in thread 816774).
- A5 | confirmed against one stale machine-dump gist: 1,303 labels; sky, blue_sky, night_sky, daytime, sunset_sunrise, mountain, water, food, people, dog present; landscape, selfie, portrait absent. Also absent: sunset, sunrise, fog, indoor (this settles the brief's two-gist disagreement in favour of "absent", against the gist I could read). What would settle it against the iOS 27 SDK: dump the supported identifiers on-device (the Revision1 `knownClassifications(forRevision:)` call named above, or the Swift request's `supportedIdentifiers`) and diff against this gist; time `perform` on a 12 MP image on the iPhone 17 Pro for latency.
