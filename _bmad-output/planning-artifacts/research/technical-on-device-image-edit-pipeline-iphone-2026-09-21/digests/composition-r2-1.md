### T3 — HDR and formats
- F10. WWDC24-10177 (iOS 18): saving an edited HDR photo. (a) edited fully in HDR: 10-bit HEIF, PQ. (b) edited as SDR **and** HDR: `writeHEIFRepresentation` with the edited SDR image + colour space and the edited HDR image via the new `HDRImage` option; **Core Image computes the gain map automatically**. (c) SDR + gain strategy: pass SDR + `HDRGainMapImage`; original gain-map metadata reused if kept. (d) ImageIO: `CGImageDestinationAddAuxiliaryDataInfo` with `kCGImageAuxiliaryDataTypeISOGainMap`. | https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10177/ | Apple | 2024-06 | 2026-09-21 | H for iOS 18; UNCONFIRMED for iOS 26/27 | capability | yes
- F11. Same session: "once an edit has been applied in HDR, the original Gain Map can no longer be used"; the SDR+gain strategy "works best when doing simple edits such as rotations, warps, and crops"; a filter that "strongly alters the brightness of the SDR image won't have an appropriate effect when applied to the Gain Map"; the gain image is "typically half the size of the SDR image". | same | Apple | 2024-06 | 2026-09-21 | H (iOS 18) | capability | yes

## Hypotheses checked
- **H1 (masked-LUT + depth-blur in well under 1 s on iPhone 17 Pro): NOT FOUND, unmeasured.** Only component evidence: DA-V2-Small 33.9 ms at ~518x396 on an A17 Pro-class chip (F5); small nets on 17 Pro/iOS 27 run in 4-8 ms (F6, different models); ANE gained only 1-1.15x on A19 Pro vs A18 Pro in Argmax's speech test (F2), so do not extrapolate a big depth-model speedup; nothing times `CIEdgePreserveUpsampleFilter`, `CIColorCubesMixedWithMask` or a depth blur at 12-48 MP. Inference (not evidence): the ML step is small next to the full-res render, and the full-res render is where the unknown lives.
- **H2 (Adaptive-HDR look lost after an SDR-background composite unless two parallel composites): PARTLY.** Supported: Apple's write API takes an edited SDR image **and** an edited HDR image and computes the gain map itself (F10); original gain map is unusable once edited in HDR (F11); brightness-altering edits cannot be mirrored onto the gain map, which is exactly what a new background + relit subject does (F11); many CI ops return headroom 0/unknown (F12), and some blend modes are not HDR-capable. Not supported/unmeasured: that the result visibly "loses its HDR look" — no measurement or complaint found. Nuance: "two parallel composites" is the API's shape (SDR composite + HDR composite); deriving the SDR one from a single HDR composite with `CIToneMapHeadroom` is an untested idea of mine. An SDR diffusion background has no highlight headroom (values <= 1.0), so it will sit flatter than the subject's HDR highlights (inference). Validity on iOS 26/27 unconfirmed.
- **H3 (grain/sharpness matching as important as colour matching): NOT FOUND (unranked).** Sources treat noise/blur matching as a recognised part of harmonization (F18) but none ranks it against colour; the ranking study (Xue et al., "Understanding and improving the realism of image composites", ACM TOG 2012, doi 10.1145/2185520.2185580) surfaced in search but was not read. Structural argument (inference): a 512-1024 px diffusion background upscaled to 12 MP is smoother and grain-free next to a sensor-original subject, so the mismatch is systematic, not incidental.

## Techniques / shipped approaches not on your list
- Apple's native **SDR+HDR pair write** (`HDRImage` option, gain map computed by Core Image): the sanctioned way to export without a hand-built gain map (F10).
- Draw Things **High-Res Fix in latent space** (first pass small, x1.5/x2 latent upscale skipping decode/encode) + **tiled VAE decode**: a shipped on-device answer to "generate small, deliver bigger" (F16); still needs a pixel-space step to reach 12 MP.
- **Multi-scale (pyramid) harmonization** of contrast/texture/noise/blur (Sunkavalli 2010): deterministic, cheap, and implementable in Core Image/Metal; a non-ML alternative or complement to MKL-Harmonizer/PCT-Net for step 3 (F18).
- Frequency-separation / detail re-injection from the original photo (low frequencies from generated, high from original): a standard idea I know but did NOT find sourced this run — unverified.
- Finding to keep: Core AI gave no speed advantage over Core ML INT8 on 17 Pro for small vision nets (F6); choose the runtime on packaging/memory, not speed.
