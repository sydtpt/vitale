- Swift-native `GenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest` exists; availability iOS 18.0+, iPadOS 18.0+, macOS 15.0+, tvOS 18.0+, visionOS 2.0+, watchOS 27.0+; returns `InstanceMaskObservation` | https://developer.apple.com/documentation/vision/generateforegroundinstancemaskrequest (via sosumi.ai) | Apple | n/d | 2026-09-21 | H | version | no. - The iOS 17 ObjC-style `VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest` is class-agnostic: "Any foreground object, regardless of its semantic class, can be potentially segmented" (people, pets, buildings, food, shoes, vehicles named) | https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10176 (via sosumi.ai) | Apple WWDC23 "Lift subjects from images in your app" | 2023-06 | M-H | capability | no.

It covers DetectFaceRectangles, RecognizeText, DetectBarcodes, GenerateObjectnessBasedSaliency, the new CalculateImageAestheticsScores and holistic body pose; "Vision has 31 different requests" | https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2024/10163 (via sosumi.ai) | Apple | 2024-06 | M | capability | no. ## Hypotheses checked
- **H1 — partly confirmed.** Prompt-free and class-agnostic: confirmed (WWDC23, iOS 17 API; Swift-native since iOS 18). Empty result when there is no foreground subject: documented, but no independent test on landscapes and no evidence on "near-empty" partial masks. Unverified and therefore open: quality on hair/thin structures, latency, memory, and person + held-object merging. Treat it as the zero-weights first try, and design the pipeline so an empty or implausible mask (e.g. near-full-frame or tiny) routes to "skip segmentation / whole-image restyle".
- **H2 — partly, and weak for this user's photo mix.** Depth is captured automatically on iPhone 15+, but per secondary sources only when a person, cat or dog is detected — so for landscapes, streets, food, objects and sky there is nothing embedded. For selfie/companion/pet frames a portrait/semantic matte may exist at zero inference cost, but (a) which auxiliary layers an auto-depth photo actually carries, (b) whether HEIC portraits reliably expose the portrait effects matte (forum lead), (c) the 48 MP no-depth hint, and (d) survival through edits/exports are all unverified. Verdict: opportunistic shortcut for person/pet frames, not a general mechanism.
- **H3 — not found.** The SAM 3 lite recipe README has no latency/RAM figure; searches found only H200 numbers and generic "iPhone inference is fast" copy. Round 1's disk sizes (430 vs 623 MB from sessions 325/326) were not re-verified.

## Leads worth chasing
1. Read Apple Developer Forums thread 677575 and WWDC19 sessions 225/260 in full: exactly which mattes an Apple-Camera HEIC carries and under what capture conditions; then test on a handful of the user's own iPhone 15+/17 Pro files with `CGImageSourceCopyAuxiliaryDataInfoAtIndex` (a 10-minute experiment beats more searching, and settles auto-depth vs portrait-only vs 48 MP).
2. Read the `efficient-sam` and `depth-anything` recipe READMEs in `apple/coreai-models` (sam3's had no numbers; these may) and the WWDC26 326/325 pages for an on-device latency table.
3. Depth Anything v3 licence per size — Apple's recipe uses v3, so the V2 "Small = Apache-2.0" result may not transfer; this is a publishing-blocker candidate.
4. Meta SAM 3 licence text (HF-gated "SAM License": redistribution of converted weights inside or alongside an app) — the main publishing question for SAM 3 lite.
5. Meta SAM 3.1 blog and arXiv 2603.11917: size, licence, whether Apple's recipe tracks it.
6. Segmentation approaches not on the brief's list, worth a look for general daytime photos (unverified suggestions, no source read): (a) Vision's person-specific requests (`VNGeneratePersonSegmentationRequest` / `VNGeneratePersonInstanceMaskRequest`, iOS 15/17) for selfies and companions; (b) a **sky-only mask** (capture-time sky matte if it exists, or a sky segmenter) — daytime outdoor photos nearly always have sky, the region is easy, and "restyle the sky/background only" needs no subject; (c) **depth-threshold near/far separation** from Depth Anything V2 Small (Apache-2.0) or Apple's DA3 recipe, which still works on subject-less landscapes where object masks return nothing; (d) SAM 3 with the concept prompt chosen by the same rules/small LM that picks the preset (e.g. "person", "dog", "sky").
7. Alpha refinement for hair/edges (ViTMatte, MODNet, MatAnyone) — still unread.
