## Strongest counter-argument

The conclusion welds four separate claims into one: (a) the model runs on an iPhone, (b) it preserves layout and faces, (c) the owner accepts the result, (d) it exports at 12-48 MP. The evidence I could retrieve undercuts each. For (a), no one has shipped klein 4B EDIT on an iPhone. The best open runtime ships text-to-image only, keeps edit on a slower MLX path measured at 36 s for a 512² edit on a Mac, and hit thermal pressure at 1024² on an iPhone 17 Pro; a separate Core AI port measured a ~6.5 GB peak against a ~6.1 GB per-process limit. For (b) and (c), klein is a step-and-guidance-distilled 4B model whose card is silent on identity, with reports of waxy skin, a cottage industry of consistency add-ons (nearly all for 9B), a "soft light rewrites the face" prompt failure, and small faces in a 512² downsample that a diffusion model simply does not have the pixels to keep. The bar for (c) is also unusually high: relatives notice a nose that is 5% off in a family photo. For (d), the expansion is 61x to 244x in pixels, and Adobe users have complained about exactly this seam (1024 px stretched over a big photo loses micro-contrast and sensor noise). The only approach that reliably survives is to stop treating the generated image as the export: use it as a low-frequency guide (tone, colour, look) applied to the untouched full-resolution original, or composite a generated background behind a full-resolution subject matte, and never let the model repaint faces. At that point what ships is closer to classical grading and compositing with an AI-derived look than to "restyle with a diffusion model", and for "illustration" restyles that repaint texture the conclusion probably fails outright.

## Verdict

**Partly survives; needs four revisions.**
- Global tone/colour looks ("golden hour", "film" as colour grade) and background replacement via matte are plausible if the generated image is used as a guide or background plate and the full-resolution original supplies the subject. That reading is not refuted.
- "On an iPhone" for klein 4B edit is unproven as of 2026-09-21. Downgrade to "feasible in principle (2.6 GiB footprint claimed by one runtime; t2i works on an A19 Pro), not demonstrated for edit, thermally unproven at 1024²". Keep a Mac/cloud fallback in the plan.
- "Faces preserved well enough for the owner to accept" is unsupported by any independent 4B edit evidence. Restrict the claim to protection by construction (face/person mask, original pixels composited back) rather than model fidelity, and add an owner-acceptance test on real photos before asserting it.
- "Brought to full resolution acceptably" holds only for low-frequency transfers. Texture-changing looks (illustration, film grain) at 12-48 MP need a separate detail source (original pixels, synthetic grain) or should be limited to a lower export size.
- The SD 1.5 + few-step LoRA branch has no positive evidence in what I found and inherits the low-strength blur/artifact complaint (unchecked for staleness); treat it as weaker than klein for faces.
